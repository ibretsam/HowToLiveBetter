## Mục 1, 2: Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006)
- Đã mở: <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1> (302 → linkinghub.elsevier.com, trang nội dung chỉ trả Redirecting); <https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/DOI:10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1>; <https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1>.
- Tiêu đề khớp: có. Crossref: Implementation Intentions and Goal Achievement: A Meta-analysis of Effects and Processes, Advances in Experimental Social Psychology vol. 38, pp. 69–119, 2006.
- Căn cứ số liệu (nguyên văn tóm tắt Semantic Scholar): “Findings from 94 independent tests showed that implementation intentions had a positive effect of medium-to-large magnitude (d = .65) on goal attainment. Implementation intentions were effective in promoting the initiation of goal striving, the shielding of ongoing goal pursuit from unwanted influences, disengagement from failing courses of action, and conservation of capability for future goal striving.”

## Mục 3: Arkes & Blumer (1985)
- Đã mở: <https://doi.org/10.1016/0749-5978(85)90049-4> (→ linkinghub chỉ trả Redirecting); <https://api.openalex.org/works/doi:10.1016/0749-5978(85)90049-4> (xác nhận tiêu đề, tác giả, tạp chí, năm, tóm tắt rỗng); <https://www.sciencedirect.com> (403/CAPTCHA); <https://r.jina.ai/https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e4564b88ca2349962a707b76be4c75076ad6bd43> (tóm tắt); <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2796842/> (tập, trang trong trích dẫn 35, 124–140).
- Tiêu đề khớp: có. The psychology of sunk cost, Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes, 1985.
- Căn cứ số liệu, nguyên văn tóm tắt: “In a field study, customers who had initially paid more for a season subscription to a theater series attended more plays during the next 6 months, presumably because of their higher sunk cost in the season tickets”. Số buổi cụ thể từng nhóm (như 4.11 buổi) **chưa xác nhận**, mục sách đánh dấu chờ kiểm chứng, không ghi số.

## Mục 4, 8: Buehler, Griffin & Ross (1994)
- Đã mở: <https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.67.3.366> (→ psycnet, 403); <https://api.openalex.org/works/doi:10.1037/0022-3514.67.3.366> (xác nhận tiêu đề, tạp chí, có tóm tắt); PDF toàn văn <https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/biases/67_J_Personality_and_Social_Psychology_366,_1994.pdf> (trích bằng pdftotext cục bộ).
- Tiêu đề khớp: có. Trang đầu PDF: Journal of Personality and Social Psychology 1994, Vol. 67, No. 3, 366-381.
- Căn cứ số liệu (Study 1 trong toàn văn): “respondents predicted, on average, that they would finish in 33.9 days, but they actually took 55.5 days … Fewer than one third of the respondents (29.7%) finished in the time they reported as their most accurate prediction.”
- Căn cứ số liệu (Study 4 trong toàn văn): “…in the recall-relevant condition (60.0%) than in the recall and control conditions (38.1% and 29.3%, respectively)”; “Note, n = 41, 42, and 40 in the control, recall, and recall-relevant conditions”; tóm tắt: “In Study 4, the bias was eliminated for participants instructed to connect past experiences with their predictions.”
- Căn cứ số liệu (Study 2 trong toàn văn, mục 8): “A subset of the subjects (n = 62) reported having external deadlines … a majority of these subjects (80.6%) finished the projects in time to meet their deadlines … only 38.7% of these subjects finished in the predicted time … their reported completion times were strongly associated with the deadlines (r = .82, p < .001)”; dự đoán với hạn chót chỉ r = .23.
